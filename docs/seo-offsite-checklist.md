# Off-site checklist for the owner

Do this after the site is on https://www.sysnet-ksa.com. The markets are Saudi Arabia, Riyadh, and Dammam. Use one business name, one address, and one phone everywhere: Sysnet, Malaz, Riyadh, Saudi Arabia, +966 53 298 0149. Set the Google service area to Riyadh and Dammam. Do not open a second Dammam listing until you have a Dammam street and someone at that door. A listing with a city and no door number can be suspended.

- Name: Sysnet. Legal name in the description: Adam Al Arabia Company.
- Category: start with Security system supplier, then add relevant secondaries such as Fire alarm supplier and Low voltage contractor if Google offers them.

- Phone: +966 53 298 0149. Hours: Saturday–Thursday 09:00–18:00. Friday closed.
- Website: https://www.sysnet-ksa.com/en
- Photos: the storefront, the desk, and installed work you have permission to show. Do not upload stock photos of other cities.
- Description: cameras, fire alarm, access control, and cabling in Riyadh and Dammam, Saudi Arabia.
- Services: add the same 11 lines as the website, with the same names.
- Posts and Q&A: answer in English and Arabic. Point to the matching `/en/services/...` or `/ar/services/...` page.

## Google Search Console and Bing

- Verify the domain property in Google Search Console. The site reads `GOOGLE_SITE_VERIFICATION` if you want the HTML meta tag.
- Submit https://www.sysnet-ksa.com/sitemap.xml
- Inspect `/en`, `/ar`, and one service URL in each language. Confirm the HTML title is in that language before you request indexing.
- In Bing Webmaster Tools, import the site from Search Console or add the same sitemap.
- Optional: set `NEXT_PUBLIC_GA_ID` for GA4. The tag loads only when that variable is set. Form sends, phone taps, and WhatsApp taps are events (`generate_lead`, `phone_click`, `whatsapp_click`).

## Bing Places and Apple Business Connect

- Bing Places: the same name, address, phone, hours, and website as Google.
- Apple Business Connect: the same details again. Apple is what iPhone maps use.

## Directories

Use the same NAP. Skip any directory that wants a keyword-stuffed name.

- Saudi, Riyadh, and Dammam directories you already trust.
- List the headquarters as Riyadh and the service area as Riyadh and Dammam. A directory that wants a Dammam street you do not have is the wrong listing.

## Reviews

- Ask after a handover, not before the system is tested.
- Send people to the Google profile. Do not offer a discount for a review.
- Reply to reviews in the language the customer wrote.
- Keep a record of the job (district and service) so a review you answer matches a real visit.

## What is still missing in the site data

- Street and building, not only Malaz.
- Latitude and longitude of that door. Schema has no geo pin until you provide one.
- Postal code.
- Social profiles (`sameAs`). None are on the site.
- A photo of the actual office front.
- Confirmation of which cities outside Riyadh a crew will really visit.
